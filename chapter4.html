7. Answer the following questions

a. Define a general-purpose computer. Give examples.

Answer: A general-purpose computer is a computer designed to perform a wide variety of tasks by using different software programs. It can be used for word processing, internet browsing, calculations, and many other tasks.

Examples: Desktop computer, Laptop computer.

b. Differentiate between an analog and a digital computer.

c. What is a hybrid computer? List examples.

Answer: A hybrid computer is a computer that combines the features of both analog and digital computers. It can process both continuous and discrete data.

Examples: ECG machine, ICU patient monitoring system, Petrol pump.

d. What is a supercomputer? List its applications.

Answer: A supercomputer is the largest, fastest, and most powerful computer. It is used to perform very complex calculations at extremely high speed.

Applications:

Weather forecasting.

Scientific research.

Space exploration.

Nuclear research.

e. What is a desktop computer?

Answer: A desktop computer is a personal computer designed to be used on a desk or table. It usually consists of a monitor, CPU, keyboard, and mouse. It is commonly used in homes, schools, and offices.

f. Why is a laptop known as a portable computer?

Answer: A laptop is known as a portable computer because it is lightweight, compact, has a built-in rechargeable battery, and can be easily carried and used one place to another.
