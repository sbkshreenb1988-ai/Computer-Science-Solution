6. Give short answers to the following questions

a. What is the key feature of the first generation computer? Ans:- The key feature of the first-generation computer was the use of vacuum tubes.

b. Which language was used in the first generation computer? Ans:- Machine language.

c. Was the second generation computer faster than the first-generation computer? Ans:- Yes, the second-generation computer was faster than the first-generation computer.

d. When was the second generation computer introduced? Ans:- The second-generation computer was introduced between 1956 and 1963.

e. Which language was used in the second generation computer? Ans:- Assembly language.

f. In which computer was the CUI-based operating system used? Ans:- The third-generation computer used a CUI (Character User Interface)-based operating

system.

g. Which generation of computers is used in today's world? Ans:- Fourth-generation computers are commonly used in today's world.

h. When was the concept of network and the Internet introduced? Ans:- The concept of network and the Internet was introduced during the fourth generation of

computers.

- First Generation computer.

- Second Generation computer

- Third Generation computer.

- Fourth Generation computer.

- Fifth Generation computer.(Present and Beyond)
